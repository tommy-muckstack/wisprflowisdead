FAQs

Good questions.

Is it actually free?

Yes. No trial, no tiers, no word caps. It’s open source under Apache-2.0.

What Mac do I need?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple silicon. Smarter cleanup and Transforms use Apple’s on-device model on macOS 26; older systems get rule-based cleanup.

What permissions does it ask for?

Microphone to hear you, Accessibility to type into other apps and read the selected text, and optionally System Audio for the Notetaker.

Why is there a login page?

One yourappisdead.com account works across every IsDead app. The Mac app itself doesn’t need it.

Is this affiliated with Wispr Flow?

No. WisprFlowIsDead is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Wispr AI or Wispr Flow.
