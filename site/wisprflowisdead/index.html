How it works

Speak at the speed you think, in the apps on your Mac.

Speak naturally

Ums, false starts and “actually, no” corrections are tidied away. Lists turn into lists. Your meaning stays, the filler goes.

so um let’s meet tuesday at two actually no three and bring the uh the deck
Let’s meet Tuesday at 3, and bring the deck.

Your Mac edits as you speak

Select text to transform it. Flexible rewriting needs Apple’s model on a compatible macOS 26 Mac. Older Macs use basic cleanup and prompt templates.

Hey just wanted to check in on whether you maybe had a chance to look at the thing I sent over last week
Did you get a chance to look at what I sent last week?

FAQs

Good questions.

Is it actually free?

Yes. No trial, no tiers, no word caps. It’s open source under Apache-2.0.

What Mac do I need?

macOS 14.2 Sonoma or later on Apple silicon. Smarter cleanup and Transforms need Apple’s on-device model available on a compatible macOS 26 Mac; older systems get rule-based cleanup.

What permissions does it ask for?

Microphone to hear you, Accessibility to type into other apps and read the selected text, and optionally System Audio for the Notetaker.

Why is there a login page?

One yourappisdead.com account works across every IsDead app. The Mac app itself doesn’t need it.

Is this affiliated with Wispr Flow?

No. WisprFlowIsDead is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Wispr AI or Wispr Flow.
