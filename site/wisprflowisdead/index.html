Questions

The Mac app inserts text into the focused app with Accessibility permission. You can also copy its result. The browser demo writes into its own editor; copy that text into another app. The Mac app needs macOS 14.2 or later on Apple silicon.

The clone combines dictation with filler removal, punctuation, formatting, a personal dictionary and snippets. Browser cleanup uses local English rules. The Mac app can use Apple’s on-device model for richer cleanup on compatible macOS 26 systems. Results still need review.

The browser demo offers eight language settings. Recognition depends on your browser and its speech provider; local cleanup is intended for English and preserves other languages. The Mac app’s language support depends on the selected speech engine. Automatic language detection is not available in the browser demo.

Accuracy varies with your speech engine, language and recording conditions. Choose the language you are speaking, try a short passage and review the transcript. Add recurring spelling corrections to your personal dictionary. The clone has not been benchmarked across accents.

Try a short recording at your normal speaking pace. Moving closer to the microphone and reducing background noise can help. The browser demo includes a microphone level check in Settings. Recognition quality depends on your equipment and speech provider; whispering and noisy audio are not guaranteed.

No extra hardware is required if your computer already has a working microphone. Allow microphone access when prompted. You can check the default input in the browser demo’s Settings; select a different input through your browser or system settings.

The Mac app processes audio locally. Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser provider’s service. Browser history and dictionary storage are optional and stay in that browser profile; they do not sync through your account. Review shared-device storage before saving sensitive text.

The clone is free and open source under Apache-2.0, with no trial, subscription or app-imposed word limit. A compatible Mac is required for the native app. Signed builds are not yet available; source-build instructions are in the download section.

Each person can install the Mac app or use the browser demo. Shared dictionaries, centralized administration, enterprise SSO and team synchronization are not implemented. Saved browser preferences and text belong to that browser profile, not a shared team account.

The Mac app includes a Notetaker that records your microphone and optional system audio. It creates a transcript with You/Others tracks and a summary. Summary quality depends on available on-device features. The browser demo is for dictation and does not record meetings.

Both are part of the free Mac app; there is no separate paid add-on. The Mac app does not require an account. Website login uses a shared yourappisdead.com account. This independent project is not affiliated with Wispr AI or Wispr Flow.
