Security and privacy.
On every plan.

In the Mac app, speech recognition and cleanup run locally. History stays on your disk until you delete it. No account is required for the Mac app.

The browser demo is separate: its speech service may send audio to the browser provider. This project does not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA certification.

Questions

No. WisprFlowIsDead has no paid plan. The cards and table above describe Wispr Flow’s reference plans, not features included in this clone. Try the browser demo or build the Mac app for free.

Yes. WisprFlowIsDead is free and open source under Apache-2.0, with dictation and the Notetaker, and no word caps. You never need a credit card.

Yes. The Notetaker records meetings from your mic and system audio, summarizes them on your Mac, and keeps the notes in a folder you own.

There is no subscription. If you ever want to stop, delete the app and the folder it keeps your history in.

It is free for everyone, including students, educators and non-profits.

There is no weekly limit. Meetings are only limited by the disk space on your Mac.

The Mac app is the one that exists today. Windows, iOS and Android are open for contributors on GitHub.

No. The Mac app captures microphone and optional system audio locally. It does not send a bot into your meeting. The separate browser dictation demo may use your browser provider’s remote speech service.

This project is independent from Wispr Flow, so it has nothing to do with your Wispr account. You can run both side by side.

You can install it on every Mac in your team. Shared dictionary, SSO and admin dashboards are not built yet, and pull requests are welcome.
